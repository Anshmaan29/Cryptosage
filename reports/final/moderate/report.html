Limits of interpretation

  • RSS samples are not a continuous peak-native-memory profiler.
  • Measurements from concurrent clients may include their worker-thread activity.
  • No network transmission latency is inferred from cryptographic computation timings.

The benchmark restricts each handshake to the KEM under test. The total includes signed policy advertisements, the admissible ephemeral KEM public key, key confirmations and key derivation.

Network measurements use actual loopback sockets. A configured artificial delay is inserted at test endpoints before every send; it is not evidence about a physical WAN. Do not subtract an opaque-payload roundtrip from a protocol exchange to claim exact transmission overhead. CPU/RSS observations cover the KEM trial process and can include other process threads; cumulative high-water RSS is not attributable to an individual algorithm.
